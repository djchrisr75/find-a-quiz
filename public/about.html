About Find a Quiz

Find a Quiz is the easy way to find a quiz night near you: your local on a Tuesday, or somewhere new when you’re away for the weekend or on holiday. Search a town, a postcode or just tap “Near me”, wherever you are in the world.

For quiz hosts and venues

List your quiz with everything players want to know: when it’s on, entry fee, team sizes, prizes, ages, food, booking details and photos. Weekly, fortnightly, monthly and one-off quizzes all work, and you can change your listing whenever you like with your private edit link.

Listing costs £2 per quiz date: pick how many upcoming dates you want shown (a weekly quiz for 8 weeks is 8 dates) and add more whenever you like. List your quiz.

“Found online” listings

To get things started we’ve added quizzes we found on public listings and venue websites, and SpeedQuizzing trivia nights across the USA from SpeedQuizzing’s public quiz finder. These are marked as found online until the venue or host claims them. If one of them is yours, open it and press “Claim it” to fix the details, add photos and get a ✓ Confirmed badge. If a quiz has stopped, use “Something wrong with this listing?” on its page and we’ll take it down.
