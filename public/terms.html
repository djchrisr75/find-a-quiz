Terms & privacy

Find a Quiz (find-a-quiz.com) is run by Max Entertainment Limited, Dudley, United Kingdom.

Listing a quiz

  • Listing costs £2 per quiz date. You choose how many upcoming dates to pay for; your listing is shown until the last of those dates, and you can add more from your edit link. If you change your quiz’s days, you keep the same number of paid dates.
  • You must run, host or work for the quiz or venue you list, and the details must be accurate. Keep them up to date with your edit link.
  • Only upload photos you took or have permission to use. No photos of children without their parents’ permission, and nothing offensive.
  • We can edit, hide or remove a listing that’s wrong, misleading, offensive or isn’t a quiz. If we remove a listing because it breaks these rules, there’s no refund.
  • If something goes wrong with your payment or listing, contact us and we’ll put it right.
  • Keep your edit link private: anyone with it can change your listing.

For players

We do our best to keep listings accurate, but quizzes change. Always check with the venue before travelling. Listings marked “found online” came from public listings and haven’t been confirmed by the venue.

Privacy

  • Listers: we keep your name, email and phone number so we can send your edit link and contact you about your listing. We never show them on the site, sell them or share them.
  • Payments are handled by SumUp. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your location: “Near me” uses your device’s location only to find nearby quizzes. It isn’t stored.
  • Searches for places are looked up with postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap. Maps come from OpenStreetMap and fonts from Google Fonts, so those services see your device’s IP address when the page loads.
  • Cookies: we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. When you list a quiz, a draft is kept in your own browser so you don’t lose your typing.
  • To have your details removed, use your edit link to take your listing down, or contact us.
